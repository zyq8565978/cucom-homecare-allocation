# Definitions, units and evidence boundaries

## Population and denominator

The frozen 2018 cohort contains 2,433 community-dwelling adults aged 60 years or older, reporting at least two chronic conditions and the qualifying functional difficulties. The nested assistance-gap group contains 564 people. Raw counts and survey-weighted percentages are different quantities. All allocation results use the original 2018 weighted population standardised to 1,000 unless explicitly stated otherwise.

## Priorities

Five basic ADL and five IADL items retain native values 1–4. Basic score B and IADL score I each sum response minus one, giving ordinal indices from 0 to 15. Four mutually exclusive priority tiers are: at least one basic ADL cannot be performed; basic ADL requires help without inability; IADL dependence without basic dependence; other qualifying functional difficulty. These are model priorities, not validated clinical disability grades.

S0-R orders tier, B and I descending. S1-R preserves tier and B, using an explicit assistance gap before I **only inside the partially allocated tier–B boundary**. This can displace IADL-heavier recipients with existing family help. It does not establish that gap recipients benefit more from treatment. Exact ties receive a common weighted allocation probability, rather than an arbitrary participant-ID tie-break.

For targeted enquiry, feasibility and the enquiry boundary are determined from function, weights and available resources before unrevealed assistance answers are accessed. Unknown assistance retains the original function-first probability. Enquiry effort is charged when performed; unsuccessful attempts to find a feasible target are not erased. Necessary care, safety assessment and referral are not represented as optional services removable by the allocation rule.

## Resources

The programme lasts five months. Planning inputs are 8 RN hours, 11.5 OT hours and 10 patient-contact hours per complete programme. Costs are 2024 US$, using US$1 = CNY7.1217 and the unrounded parameter ledger. Brief support enquiry is 5 minutes in the reference, with 3 and 10 minutes as sensitivities. It is not a statutory disability assessment.

Extra rural travel is 0.5 hours per visit, applied separately to four RN and six OT visits. The shared historical hourly price is a planning reference, not an observed estimate of both professions' marginal wages; separate RN and OT price coefficients are released. Family programme participation of 0, 5 or 10 hours and synchronous enquiry fractions are scenarios, not measured caregiver relief.

## Comparisons

Equal programme numbers with support already known exclude acquisition cost. Equal programme numbers with paid enquiry require extra RN time/cost. Fixed-resource comparisons cap RN, OT and budget jointly. At the 500-programme reference these ceilings are 4,000 RN hours, 5,750 OT hours and US$539,277.9232398866. Expansion without displacement uses the union of the two allocation-probability vectors and accounts for every additional complete programme; it is not a third priority rule.

For 2020, current eligible weighted mass remains on the original baseline denominator. Compare a 500-programme target, its fixed-resource counterpart and 50% of current eligible mass separately. Shortfalls and failed requested targets remain in all-draw summaries; selected successful draws do not substitute for unconditional results.

## Uncertainty and disclosure

Paired percentile 95% intervals use 5,000 community with-replacement draws, with 5,000 county draws as sensitivity; seed 20261027. Intervals are pointwise, not simultaneous. The same draw is used across policies and capacities. A bootstrap fraction is a sampling-stability summary, not the posterior probability of benefit. Grid rows are computed capacities, not interpolated switching points.

Small exchanged groups and reconstructable complements are withheld according to the frozen release rules. In particular, some S1 differences at capacities 100, 200 and 700 remain withheld. The public binned trade-off histogram describes existing aggregate draws, not participant trajectories.

## Evidence

CHARLS informs observed functional need, assistance, family context, residence and follow-up status. Allocation and costs are model outputs under planning rules. CAPABLE supplies the full-service clinical framework, not an identified China-specific treatment effect. Service opportunity is not health gain; IADL need coverage loss is not measured deterioration. No positive social net benefit, QALY or caregiver-hour saving is inferred from coverage alone.
