# Data availability and access

The repository shares disclosure-controlled aggregate results and the numerical data underlying the study tables and figures. These are derived research outputs, not CHARLS participant-level microdata. Aggregate CSV files preserve the frozen estimates, confidence intervals, denominators and suppression rules. Numerical zero, blank, unknown, unavailable and withheld values must remain distinct.

The underlying 2018 and 2020 participant data are maintained by the China Health and Retirement Longitudinal Study (CHARLS), Peking University. Researchers must obtain access through the [official CHARLS website](https://charls.pku.edu.cn/en/) and comply with the provider's current registration, access and data-use conditions. This repository does not grant access to or permission to redistribute those data.

Local analytical files, identifiers, sampling-unit labels, individual weights, linked records and individual allocation probabilities are not distributed. The complete 5,000-draw arrays are also excluded; released confidence intervals and a disclosure-controlled binned aggregate trade-off display are included.

The MIT software licence covers original code, not third-party datasets, questionnaires, publications or their contents. No blanket open-data licence is asserted for CHARLS data. Users should cite the study and the original CHARLS source when using the aggregate results, and respect the existing suppression rules.
